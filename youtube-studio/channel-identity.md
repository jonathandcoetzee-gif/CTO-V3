# YouTube Channel Identity Templates

This document outlines the branding, voice, and affiliate strategies for the four initial channels in the network.

## 1. WealthMind AI (Finance/Wealth)
- **Channel Name**: WealthMind AI
- **Brand Voice**: Authoritative yet accessible. Focuses on actionable wealth-building strategies, market analysis, and psychological aspects of money. Tone is professional, forward-looking, and highly trustworthy.
- **ElevenLabs Voice**: "Marcus" (Deep, calm, professional male)
- **Thumbnail Guide**: 
    - **Colors**: Navy Blue, Gold, and Crisp White.
    - **Font**: Bold Sans-Serif (e.g., Montserrat or Anton).
    - **Composition**: High-quality stock footage of modern architecture, luxury items, or clean financial charts with large, high-contrast text overlays.
- **Affiliate Stack**:
    1. **TradingView** (Charts/Analysis) - 30% recurring
    2. **NordVPN** (Security for investors) - 40% commission
    3. **Ledger** (Crypto security) - 10% per sale
    4. **MasterClass** (Business/Finance courses) - 25% per sale
    5. **Audible** (Wealth-focused books) - $15 per signup

## 2. GoldenYears Guide (Senior Health)
- **Channel Name**: GoldenYears Guide
- **Brand Voice**: Gentle, encouraging, and respectful. Focuses on longevity, simple exercises, nutrition for seniors, and mental well-being. Tone is warm, patient, and community-oriented.
- **ElevenLabs Voice**: "Dorothy" or "Arthur" (Warm, mature, steady)
- **Thumbnail Guide**: 
    - **Colors**: Soft Greens, Sky Blue, and Warm Peach.
    - **Font**: Friendly Serif (e.g., Playfair Display).
    - **Composition**: Bright, natural lighting. Images of active seniors smiling, healthy vibrant food, or serene outdoor settings.
- **Affiliate Stack**:
    1. **SilverSneakers** (Fitness) - Lead-based commission
    2. **Seed Probiotics** (Digestive health) - 25% commission
    3. **BetterHelp** (Mental health) - $100 per conversion
    4. **Amazon Associates** (Mobility aids/Supplements) - 4-10% commission
    5. **Yoga Burn for Seniors** - 50% commission

## 3. Justice Told (Betrayal/Revenge)
- **Channel Name**: Justice Told
- **Brand Voice**: Gripping, dramatic, and slightly mysterious. Focuses on real-life stories of overcoming betrayal and achieving "poetic justice." Tone is suspenseful, empathetic to the victim, and satisfying in conclusion.
- **ElevenLabs Voice**: "Ethan" (Gravely, narrative-driven male)
- **Thumbnail Guide**: 
    - **Colors**: Deep Red, Black, and High-Contrast White.
    - **Font**: Impactful, slightly distressed (e.g., Impact or Bebas Neue).
    - **Composition**: Dramatic, split-screen silhouettes, dark backgrounds with one highlighted element (like a letter or a broken ring).
- **Affiliate Stack**:
    1. **Social Catfish** (Identity/Relationship verification) - 30% commission
    2. **BeenVerified** (Background checks) - $35 per signup
    3. **TruthFinder** - $35 per signup
    4. **Incogni** (Data privacy) - 35% commission
    5. **Online-Therapy.com** - 20% recurring

## 4. FluentFast AI (English Learning)
- **Channel Name**: FluentFast AI
- **Brand Voice**: High-energy, supportive, and clear. Focuses on practical phrases, common mistakes, and immersive learning. Tone is educational, fast-paced, and globally inclusive.
- **ElevenLabs Voice**: "Serena" (Clear, neutral American accent, upbeat)
- **Thumbnail Guide**: 
    - **Colors**: Bright Yellow, Royal Blue, and White.
    - **Font**: Clean, rounded Sans-Serif (e.g., Quicksand or Poppins).
    - **Composition**: Action-oriented text (e.g., "STOP SAYING THIS"), clear flags/symbols, and expressive AI avatars or stock faces.
- **Affiliate Stack**:
    1. **Italki** (1-on-1 tutoring) - $15 per new student
    2. **Preply** - 100% of first lesson fee
    3. **Babbel** - Up to $32 per signup
    4. **Grammarly** - $0.20 per signup / $20 per upgrade
    5. **ELSA Speak** (Pronunciation app) - 25-40% commission
